- New: the app library of paddock for Mac. You can add an app by a drop on the window or on the Dock icon, and paddock's dry run shows as a checklist before the add. The library lists the instances of each app and the suspended sessions. Settings > Storage shows `paddock du` and a preview of `paddock gc` before anything is removed. The launcher offers to copy itself into `~/Applications`.
- Changed: paddock for Mac reads the JSON that paddock 0.2.x prints for `app list`, `app add`, `app remove`, `macvm status`, `macvm setup` and the `golden`, `disk`, `setup` and `stream` events. It needs paddock 0.2.2 or newer and compares the patch number too.
- Fix: paddock for Mac calls `app run` without `--json`, `app remove` and `macvm remove` with `--yes`, `app add` with `--memory 6G` and without `--network`, and `macvm setup` with `--yes`, as the CLI takes them.
