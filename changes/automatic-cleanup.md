- New: At the start of `paddock run`, an automatic cleanup runs in the background, at most once an hour. It removes merged worktrees without uncommitted changes, also after a cherry-pick or a rebase. It deletes the build output of worktrees that were not used for 2 days. It removes old snapshots and the homes of gone projects. `[storage] auto_gc = false` turns it off.
- New: When a `--worktree` run or a spawned worker ends without a commit and without a change, paddock removes its worktree and its branch.
- New: Below a hard floor of free space (15 GB or 5%), paddock runs the cleanup and then refuses to start a sandbox. Below a soft floor (50 GB or 10%), it warns. While sandboxes run, it sends a notification at the hard floor. `paddock doctor` shows the free space, the floors and the last cleanup.
- New: Worktree runs and workers of a Rust project build outside the worktree. Each one gets a copy-on-write copy of the newest build of the same repository. `[setup] shared_build_cache = false` in `paddock.toml` turns it off for a project.
- Changed: `paddock worktree list` shows the size, the last use and the merged state of each worktree. `paddock du` shows the build caches.
