- Fix: In network mode `ask` of the macOS guest, only the app asks. The guest system never asks: a daemon, a widget or an Apple service that no rule allows is denied at once with the rule word `system`. paddock logs each such connection with its host and process, and counts it per host. Before this, the guest's own services filled the screen with dozens of panels in the first minute.
- Changed: A session shows one firewall panel at a time. The other questions wait behind it, and the panel says how many. At most three questions come up per minute. A panel never closes by itself while its question is open. An answer that comes after the connection timed out counts for the next connection to that host.
- New: When more than 20 questions of a session are open, asking pauses. One line goes to the session log. `paddock app status NAME` says `asking paused` and lists the open questions and the denied guest system hosts. One notice offers `Deny all` or `Ask one by one`.
- Changed: The firewall panel and the badge of the window marker use the system popover material and the system text colors. The text and the buttons are visible in Light and Dark mode. The panel has `Deny` (Esc), `Allow once` and `Always allow` (Return), and the other answers under `More…`.
- New: The golden turns off guest services that phone home: desktop widgets, Siri and suggestions, the iCloud setup nag, the News, Weather and Siri agents, automatic macOS installs, location services, analytics and personalized ads. A setting that does not take is a warning of the setup, not a failed golden.
- Changed: The Mac check scripts run their scratch sessions with `--network none` or `--network egress`, so no firewall question waits for a person. `macvm-firewall.sh` is the one script that shows panels, and it says so first.
