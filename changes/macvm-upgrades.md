- New: `paddock macvm update [--ipsw PATH] [--force] [--yes]` builds a new golden macOS image next to the current one and swaps them when it is sealed. The old golden stays as `golden.prev` until a profile completes a session on the new one, then `paddock gc` lists it. `paddock macvm rollback` puts the previous golden back while it exists. A swap that a crash interrupted between its two folder renames is finished at the next `paddock app run`, `macvm status`, `update` or `rollback`, with one line. Each profile clones the new golden at its next start, after a `pre-golden-<version>` backup of its data disk; the data disk and the app copy stay.
- New: the guest agent of paddock's macOS guest updates itself from the session's `agent` share, so a paddock upgrade or downgrade never needs a golden rebuild. `paddock app run --backend macvm` makes the share `macvm/agent/<version>/` from `paddock-guest` next to `paddock-vm` and checks its hash file; the Homebrew formula signs the agent and writes the hash file. When the new agent fails three times at once, the guest falls back to its previous agent, and the session logs `agent rollback` and talks to it without a second update.
- New: `paddock macvm status` has a `prev` line for the previous golden, `paddock doctor` a `golden.prev` line, and `paddock macvm status --json` a `prev` object.
- Changed: `paddock macvm update` without `--force` refuses an image of the version the golden already runs, before any download.
