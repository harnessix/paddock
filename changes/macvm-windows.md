- New: on the macOS guest route, every window of the app in the guest is a borderless host window of its own: a `VZVirtualMachineView` of the full guest display, cropped to that window, so the window follows the guest's moves, resizes, titles, order, minimize and focus, and a click, a key or Cmd-Tab on the host reaches the guest window. Menus, popovers, tooltips, sheets and the candidate window of an input method are child windows of their parent and never take key. A guest full screen and a window above `normal` that is larger than its level allows are refused with a badge and a move back. After an agent restart, the host windows that still match keep their place, so nothing flickers. The session has an app menu, a `Window` menu and a Dock menu with the windows, `Open <App> again`, `Restart guest agent`, `Restart guest` after 30 s of silence, and `Quit`, and Cmd-Q runs the quit path. The control socket of the session gets `window move`, `window raise`, `window list` and the `bench` lines that the Mac check `packaging/macos/checks/macvm-windows.sh` drives.
