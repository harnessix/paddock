- New: `paddock macvm setup` builds the golden macOS image of app mode on a Mac with Apple silicon: the restore image from Apple (or `--ipsw PATH`), the install with `VZMacOSInstaller`, the seed of the guest agent's launch script and launchd plist, one guided step in a paddock window, and the seal (mode 0444 and `uchg` on the images). The setup records its state in `manifest.toml` and continues where it stopped, also after a crash: a half-written file is removed and its step runs again. It checks the free space before the download and before the install, and keeps the password of the guest account in paddock's keyring.
- New: `paddock macvm status` shows the golden, the host version, the profiles and the sessions; `paddock macvm remove [--profiles]` removes the golden, and the profiles after a copy of each data disk into the trash folder. `paddock doctor` on macOS gains a `macvm` block.
- The guided step waits for the guest agent: its `hello` marks the first three steps of the panel done, its health report with the Accessibility permission the fourth. The configure step sends the list of `src/macvm/configure.rs` to the agent and checks every result against the step's expected text. The seal asks the agent to shut the guest down.
- The framework calls run in `paddock-vm macvm-setup`, the helper with the Virtualization entitlement (`packaging/macos/paddock-vm.entitlements`, unchanged: it covers macOS guests too).
