- New: (experimental) horse DNA. Every horse has a DNA, a TOML document with traits, policies and capabilities. The capabilities are recorded only and grant nothing. The first entry of every new session tells the model the DNA of its horse. A child horse of a plain spawn inherits the DNA of its parent.
- New: (experimental) `paddock horse dna set HORSE FILE` hot-loads a DNA: each running session of the horse gets it as one `system` entry at its next safe point. Inside the sandbox, a write of `/dev/paddock/horse/self/dna.toml` or `paddock-host horse dna put` does the same. `dna show` and `dna history` show the DNA and every change with its hash, and `dna revert HORSE HASH` goes back to an earlier DNA.
- New: (experimental) `paddock horse graft HOST DONOR` adds the traits of DONOR to HOST. HOST keeps its id, its sessions and its own values, and a different value of DONOR becomes an alternative. A different policy, or a capability that HOST does not have, refuses the graft. `paddock horse move SESSION HORSE` moves an idle session and grafts the DNA of its old horse, and `paddock horse guard SESSION --move HORSE` shows that a move keeps the cached prefix.
- New: (experimental) at the end of each run in horse mode, three rules set the traits `runs`, `harnesses` and `reliability`. `paddock horse explain HORSE` shows where each trait comes from, the DNA events and the lineage of each session. `paddock explain` shows the same for a run in horse mode.
