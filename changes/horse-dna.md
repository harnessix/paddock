- New: (experimental) horse DNA. Every new horse has a DNA, a TOML document in the store with traits, policies and capabilities. A plain spawn from a run in horse mode makes a child horse that inherits the DNA of its parent. A write of `/dev/paddock/horse/self/dna.toml` (or `paddock-host horse dna put`, or `paddock horse dna set HORSE FILE` on the host) hot-loads: each running session of the horse gets the new DNA as a `system` entry at its next safe point. The first entry of every new session in horse mode tells the model the DNA of its horse. `paddock horse dna show|set|history` shows the DNA, sets it, and lists every change with its hash, so `dna set HORSE HASH` restores an earlier DNA. The capabilities in a DNA are recorded only and grant nothing.
