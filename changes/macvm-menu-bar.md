- New: on the macOS guest route, the host menu bar shows the app's own menus from the guest (`File`, `Edit`, `View` and the rest, with their shortcuts, enabled and checked states, two levels deep), read over Accessibility by the guest agent, and a click on an item presses it in the guest. The paddock app menu stays first. A menu bar above 20 KiB loses whole submenus, never half a list.
- New: when the agent is slow (the `menu` request has no answer in 2 s), restarts or is away, the host keeps the last menu bar; a click while the agent is away is dropped with a log line, not sent; after the app exits the paddock menu with the `Window` menu is back.
- `paddock app status --json` has `menu` (`shown`, `items`, `away`), events.jsonl has the `menu` lines, and `packaging/macos/checks/macvm-menu.sh` checks the tree, a press, the switch between two sessions and an agent restart on a Mac.
