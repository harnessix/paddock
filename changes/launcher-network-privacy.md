- New: paddock for Mac shows the network and privacy of each app in three parts of the Grants tab. Network has the mode and a table of rules with host, port, answer, scope, who made the rule and the time left of a temporary rule. You add, edit and remove rules there, with wildcards and a switch for a whole site. Connections lists the recent connections of the session, and a denied host or its whole site is allowed with one click. Files & Clipboard shows the open files of each folder before you remove it, the folders of one session, and the clipboard of the app and of the running session.
- Changed: the Questions list of paddock for Mac offers the answers of paddock's panel in the same order: Deny, Allow once and Allow with the site, and under More the session, permanent and ten-minute answers.
