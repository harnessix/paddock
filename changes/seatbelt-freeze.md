- New: `paddock freeze` and `paddock thaw` work for Seatbelt runs on macOS. The run starts in a process group of its own, and paddock sends SIGSTOP or SIGCONT to that group and to every process in its tree until no new process shows up. Leases, the audit log, `paddock ps` and the `on_timeout = "ask"` freeze of a spawned child work as on Linux. App mode on macOS still cannot be frozen.
