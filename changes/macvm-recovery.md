- Changed: on Apple silicon the data disk of a macOS guest profile mounts only under the system disk that formatted it, so paddock keeps the two together for the life of the profile. A golden update or rollback no longer replaces the system disk of an existing profile; a repair keeps it; the plain `paddock app reset NAME` keeps it with the data disk. A profile whose system disk is gone is refused with one line that names the restore, and nothing of it changes.
- New: `paddock app backup NAME [--out DIR] [--stop]` clones the disk pair of a profile into `backups/<time>/`, `paddock app restore NAME FOLDER|TIME` puts one back, `paddock app backups NAME` lists them, and `paddock app reset NAME [--data | --all]` has its three tiers with a confirmation that names the sizes. `paddock gc` removes trash entries after 7 days and automatic backups above the newest 3 or 30 days.
- New: the failure matrix of the macOS guest as code: the health gap (the badge and a ping at 10 s, `Restart guest` at 30 s, a guest restart at 120 s), three timeouts in a row as an agent crash, the crash counter that stops the relaunch after three crashes in a minute with the crash report path, the wake handler, and the disk watch that warns at 2 GiB and skips the save at 1 GiB.
- Fix: the guest agent never partitions a data disk that has a partition table, so a data disk that does not mount is reported, not erased.
